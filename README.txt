PSLilly - chatting on Lily
==========================

STARTING
  Double-click PSLilly (the white flower) on your desktop.
  You can also find it in the Start menu.

THE FIRST TIME
  A box asks for your Name and Password.
  Tick "Remember me on this PC" and click Connect.
  After that, PSLilly logs you in by itself.

THE WINDOW
  Messages appear in the big area.
  You type in the strip at the bottom, then press Enter to send.

SENDING A MESSAGE
  To one person:     their name, a colon, then the message
                         bob: are you coming tonight?
  To a discussion:   the same, with the discussion's name
                         cafe: good morning everyone
  Quick reply:       on an empty line, type a colon (:) and PSLilly fills in
                     the name of whoever messaged you last.
  Names:             type the first few letters and press Tab to finish them.

HANDY THINGS
  - When someone sends you a private message, PSLilly's button on the
    taskbar flashes and it plays a sound.
  - Words underlined in red are misspelled. Click on one and press F7 to
    fix it (press F7 again for other suggestions).
  - To read older messages: scroll with the mouse wheel, or press Page Up /
    Page Down. Press Ctrl+End to jump back to the newest.
  - Bigger or smaller text: hold Ctrl and turn the mouse wheel.
  - Copy and paste work as usual (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V).
  - Pressing Up in the typing strip brings back what you sent before.
  - If the connection drops, PSLilly reconnects by itself.

LEAVING
  Just close the window.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
  Close PSLilly and start it again. If that doesn't help, ask whoever set
  PSLilly up for you.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
For whoever looks after this computer:
  Update:     unzip the new version and double-click Install.cmd again.
              Settings and the saved password are kept.
  Uninstall:  Settings > Apps > Installed apps > PSLilly > Uninstall.
  Installed:  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\PSLilly  (settings: lily.conf there).
  In PSLilly: type $$ and press Enter for all settings and commands.
